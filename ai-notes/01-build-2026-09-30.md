# reflection — build (2026-09-30)

Live: https://rt567.github.io/reflection/  ·  Repo: github.com/RT567/reflection (branch `main`, legacy Pages from `/`)

## What it is
Rob's brief: "just a pure black screen and call it reflection". The joke is that the only thing you see
is yourself, reflected in the black screen.

## Decisions
- `index.html` is the whole site: black `html`/`body`, no content, no scrollbars, tab title `reflection`.
- `theme-color` and `color-scheme` are black/dark so phone browser chrome goes black too.
- No favicon on purpose (anything visible would spoil the black). Keep it this empty; don't add text,
  hints, or an explanation.

## Deploy
`git push` → Pages builds in ~1 min. Linked from the rt567.github.io landing page as `reflection`.
